Privacy Policy — Ayant

Effective date: 30 June 2026 · Updated: 21 September 2026 · Русская версия

The Ayant app (“we”, “the app”) respects your privacy. This document describes what data we collect and how we use it.

The data controller is IE “Ayant” (ИП «Ayant»), Bishkek, Kyrgyz Republic. Contact details are in section 9.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name and email (when signing in with email, Google or Apple).
  • Location: approximate location — only to show the distance to venues. Used on the device; not published.
  • In-app activity: views, saves, reviews, coupon redemptions — to operate the service and for analytics.
  • Content: reviews and photos that you publish.
  • Device push token: to send notifications (can be disabled in phone settings).
  • Technical information: device type, OS version, anonymised usage statistics.
  • Instagram data (businesses only, on explicit connection): the username and identifier of the connected professional account, and your posts — caption, image, post link and date. See section 4.

2. How we use data

  • to show the feed of discounts, promotions and new items, and the distance to venues;
  • so you can save places, leave reviews, earn bonuses and receive coupons;
  • to send notifications about new offers (with your consent);
  • to improve the app through anonymised analytics.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. The service relies on these providers: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, server functions, analytics, push, storage of access tokens) and Cloudinary (image storage, including photos imported from Instagram). They process data solely to provide their services.

4. Instagram connection (for businesses)

A venue owner may connect their own Instagram professional account so they do not have to retype content they have already published: a selected post becomes a draft promotion in Ayant.

  • What we receive: the account username and identifier, and your posts — caption, image, post link, timestamp and media type. Only for the account that granted access.
  • What we do not do: we do not read other people’s accounts, we do not publish anything to Instagram, and we do not access messages, comments or insights.
  • Images: the photo of the selected post is copied to our storage (Cloudinary), because Instagram media links stop working after a few hours. The copy is used as the promotion’s image.
  • The access token is stored on our server only. It is never sent to the app and never disclosed to third parties.
  • Disconnecting: in the business cabinet (“Disconnect account”) or in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Disconnecting deletes the token. Promotions you already created remain yours and are deleted separately.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is stored while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and related data by contacting us (see “Contacts”).

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, and withdraw consent for notifications and location in your device settings.
